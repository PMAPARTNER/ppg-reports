SUMMARY & RECOMMENDATIONS
AI Analysis & Next Steps
AI-generated analysis of all data with prioritized action recommendations. (July 1, 2026 – July 31, 2026)

Belconti Resort achieved €3,985,180 in realized revenue for July 2026, reflecting a 7% growth compared to the previous year, supported by total PMS bookings generating €3,185,703 across 2,090 reservations. Digital channels drove strong engagement, recording 153,895 GA4 users, 262,315 sessions, and 32,588 Meta Ads clicks, alongside 298 call center sales from 1,367 calls. However, overall performance fell short of the budget target with an 87% budget attainment rate. Additionally, direct ad spend could not be matched to PMS bookings, leaving the blended ROAS metric unavailable for this period.

✅ Recommended Actions
  1. 1Establish tracking integration between advertising channels and PMS booking data to accurately attribute revenue and calculate blended ROAS.
  2. 2Optimize Google Ads and Meta Ads campaigns to increase high-intent traffic and bridge the gap toward the €4,579,238 monthly budget target.
  3. 3Implement conversion rate optimization strategies on the website to better monetize the 262,315 sessions and 4,048 GSC search clicks.
  4. 4Review call center sales processes to enhance the conversion rate from the incoming 1,367 inquiries.
