The property enjoys very high occupancy and solid RevPAR but currently lacks online visibility and direct booking channels. This plan outlines a low‑cost, data‑driven approach to turn existing strengths into new revenue opportunities.
📈

Executive Summary — Recommended Decision

Focus on converting the high‑occupancy base into direct bookings while introducing modest digital outreach.

  • Leverage the strong tour‑operator volume to promote a direct‑booking discount for repeat guests.
  • Test a small, cost‑controlled social‑media retargeting campaign aimed at Egyptian and UK/Ireland travelers.
  • Introduce a simple email capture on the website to nurture future direct bookings.

All actions can start with minimal spend, allowing quick assessment of ROI before scaling.
