Executive Summary

Blue Dreams Resort & Spa demonstrates a strong brand reputation with an overall average rating of 5.5/10 across 438 reviews, reflecting high guest satisfaction. However, there is a significant discrepancy in platform ratings that warrants attention:

  • Booking.com (7.3/10, 164 reviews) and HolidayCheck (5.4/10, 20 reviews) show exceptional performance, with perfect scores (10/10) in recent reviews praising hospitality, service quality, and amenities.
  • Google (4.3/10, 141 reviews) and TripAdvisor (4.2/10, 110 reviews) lag significantly, indicating potential platform-specific perception gaps or content visibility issues.

The latest reviews (all dated 2026-08-30/31) are overwhelmingly positive, with five 5-star or 10/10 ratings on Booking.com and TripAdvisor. Key recurring themes include:

  • Exceptional hospitality – Staff are repeatedly praised for going "above and beyond" (e.g., Mr. Burak’s team and the "feeling of service" highlighted by Halit).
  • Premium amenities – Highlights include sea views, sunsets, pools, food quality, and family-friendly activities.
  • Personalized service – Guests emphasize attentive staff who cater to specific needs (e.g., Natalia’s review).
  • One area for improvement – A single guest on Booking.com suggests expanding spa services and adding sports activities (Тахмина).

Key Insights:

  • Platform disparity suggests a need to investigate why Google and TripAdvisor ratings are lower despite positive sentiment in other channels.
  • August 2026 performance is exceptional, but the data does not reflect occupancy or revenue trends—critical for strategic planning.
  • Opportunities to leverage include highlighting staff excellence in marketing (e.g., testimonials) and addressing the spa/sports gap to differentiate further.

Strategic Recommendations for August 2026

To capitalize on the current positive momentum and address platform-specific challenges, the following data-driven actions are recommended:

1. Platform-Specific Reputation Management

Investigate and rectify the Google/TripAdvisor rating gap by:

  • Audit recent reviews on Google/TripAdvisor for negative sentiment patterns (e.g., missing reviews, delayed responses, or specific complaints).
  • Proactively engage with guests on these platforms to:
    • Respond to all new reviews within 24 hours with personalized thank-you messages (leveraging the positive sentiment from Booking.com).
    • Encourage satisfied guests to post reviews on Google/TripAdvisor via in-room cards or post-stay emails, highlighting their exceptional experience.
  • Optimize listings on Google/TripAdvisor to align with Booking.com’s high standards (e.g., update photos, emphasize sea views, and highlight staff awards).

2. Staff-Centric Marketing Campaign

Leverage the unanimous praise for staff to create a brand differentiator:

  • Develop a "Meet the Team" campaign featuring:
    • Short videos or photoshoots with key staff (e.g., Mr. Burak, front desk, spa team) highlighting their personalized service approach.
    • A dedicated staff spotlight section on the hotel website and OTA profiles, with quotes from recent guests (e.g., "They don’t just provide a service, but ‘serve feelings’—Halit").
  • Incentivize staff engagement with guests who leave detailed, positive reviews on all platforms, with recognition in team meetings or a "Guest Champion" award.
  • Train staff to encourage reviews during check-out, directing guests to high-impact platforms (Booking.com, Google) with clear instructions.

3. Targeted Amenity Expansion for High-Value Segments

Address the single feedback point about expanding spa and sports offerings to attract health-conscious and luxury-seeking guests:

  • Conduct a guest survey (via email or in-room) to validate demand for:
    • Premium spa packages (e.g., couples’ treatments, wellness retreats) tied to high-ADR rooms.
    • Sports activities such as yoga classes, paddleboarding, or beach volleyball, marketed as "exclusive experiences" for booking.com travelers.
  • Pilot a "Wellness & Adventure" add-on for August 2026, offering:
    • A discounted spa credit for guests booking a minimum of 5 nights.
    • A sports package (e.g., 2x paddleboarding + yoga session) for families or groups, promoted via Booking.com’s "Special Offers" tab.
  • Track performance of these add-ons to justify permanent inclusion, focusing on occupancy uplift in high-season periods.

Next Steps:

  • Schedule a reputation audit meeting with the marketing team to analyze Google/TripAdvisor gaps by August 5, 2026.
  • Allocate 10% of the marketing budget to the staff-centric campaign, with content ready by August 15.
  • Present a spa/sports pilot proposal to the GM for approval by August 10, 2026.
