SUMMARY & RECOMMENDATIONS
AI Analysis & Next Steps
AI-generated analysis of all data with prioritized action recommendations. (October 19, 2026 – October 25, 2026)

Amwaj Beach Club generated a strong booking pipeline this week with €423,665 in reservation value across 827 bookings, a 17% increase versus the same period last year, and maintained a high guest satisfaction score of 4.3 from 33 new reviews. Digital traffic was healthy with 731 users and 975 sessions on GA4, while Meta Ads delivered 3,367 clicks for a modest €51 spend. However, the property fell short of its revenue target, achieving only 91% of the €911,636 budget (actual €831,466), and blended ROAS could not be measured because ad spend did not match to PMS bookings. Google Ads data is missing, limiting full channel visibility.

✅ Recommended Actions
  1. 1Implement end‑to‑end conversion tracking (UTM parameters, GA4‑PMS linkage) so ad spend can be attributed to actual stay revenue.
  2. 2Launch a controlled Google Ads test campaign to capture demand not reached by Meta and compare cost‑per‑booking.
  3. 3Optimize the booking funnel landing pages and checkout flow to raise the session‑to‑booking conversion rate from the current GA4 session volume.
  4. 4Introduce a weekly budget pacing review that tracks actual PMS revenue against the €911,636 target and adjusts channel spend allocations accordingly.
