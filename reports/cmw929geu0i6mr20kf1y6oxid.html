How are the ratings calculated?
  • Actual platform rating: the overall (all-time) rating shown on the platform's own page, on the platform's own scale (Booking.com /10, Google /5, TripAdvisor /5, HolidayCheck /6 …). It is fetched from the platform page and cached; each row shows the review count, how it was obtained and its date.
  • This period: the average of only the new reviews received in this report's period. Each review is converted from its stored scale to the platform's scale (e.g. 4.0/5 → 8.0/10); a review with an unknown scale is left out. It is normal for it to differ from the actual platform rating: one describes all time, the other only this week.
  • Previous period and Change: the same calculation for the immediately preceding period of the same length; change = this period − previous period (same scale). "—" if the previous period had no reviews.
  • Small sample: a period rating based on fewer than 5 reviews hangs on a single review; it is flagged and should not be read on its own.
  • Avg. Rating (card above): the average of all reviews in this period normalised to a 5-point scale; platform scales are converted to a single scale before averaging.
